Slik spiller du

SipZone: party games on one phone or several devices

SipZone brings together party games for a group in the same room. Share one phone or use separate devices with SipZone Live. The content refers to alcohol and is intended for adults.

How to play

Choose a game, add the players and read the prompts. Agree on boundaries before starting and let everyone skip tasks. For SipZone Live, follow the in-game instructions to join on separate devices.
